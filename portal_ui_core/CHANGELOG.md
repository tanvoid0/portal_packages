## Unreleased

- Type ramp: `PortalTextStyles.hero` (44/w800, tracked -1.0) above `display` for the one figure a screen is about; every style now carries a `height` on one rule (1.1 figures, 1.2 headings, 1.25 subtitle, 1.5 body, 1.4 caption/overline). `emphasized(style)` is one weight heavier and 0.2 tighter, `numeric(style)` turns on tabular figures so counters and columns of amounts stop jittering. `PortalStatCard` sets its value in `numeric(title)`, or `numeric(hero)` with `hero: true`.

- `PortalGradients` on `DesignTokens.gradients`: the three gradients the fleet draws, built from the `ColorScheme` so they follow a theme change — `surfaceSheen(scheme)` (top-left `onSurface` at 4% to nothing, layered over a surface colour), `accent(scheme)` (`primary` → `primaryContainer` at 135°) and `imageScrim(scheme)` (`scrim` at 60% rising from the bottom edge, for text over an image). `PortalCard(sheen: true)` lays the sheen over its surface behind the content, every variant. Replaces the ad-hoc `LinearGradient`s in app code, gym's `subtleGradient` first.

- `PortalSemanticColors`: the status roles `ColorScheme` lacks — `success`, `warning`, `info` (each with `on*`, `*Container`, `on*Container`) plus `danger*` mirroring `error*` so status code reads one type. Defaults per brightness from fixed hues (green 145°, amber 40°, blue 215°) on the M3 error tone recipe, rotated toward the scheme's primary with `Blend.harmonize`; `buildPortalTheme` attaches it, `PortalSemanticColors.of(context)` falls back to computed defaults when a theme was hand-rolled, and `PortalThemeConfig.extensionsBuilder` overrides per brightness (a later `PortalSemanticColors` replaces the default). Success toasts now paint `success` (was `secondary`), destructive toasts `danger`; `PortalStatCard(delta:)` paints a `+`/`-` change in `success`/`danger`.

- `PortalHaptics`: the fleet's haptic vocabulary over the stdlib `HapticFeedback` — `selection`/`light`/`medium`/`heavy` plus the compound `success` (light→medium), `warning` (medium×2) and `error` (heavy→light→light), 40 ms apart. One global switch, `PortalHaptics.enabled`, set from the new `PortalThemeConfig.haptics` (default true) when a theme is built. Wired where it earns its place: segmented tabs and bottom nav click on a selection *change*, filter chips on every tap (the chip cannot know whether the consumer toggles or ignores a re-tap), the confirm dialog's confirm is medium, success/destructive toasts play success/error as they appear, and `PortalPressable(haptic:)` plays a `PortalHapticKind` before `onTap` for anything else.

- `PortalPressable`: press state for anything tappable — shrinks to the new `PortalMotion.pressScale` token (0.97) on the effects spring while held, springs back on the spatial one. It hosts the `InkWell`, so the scale follows Material's own pressed state (a press that becomes a scroll releases) and a quick tap still plays the press out before springing back; the ripple is off unless asked for. `PortalCard(onTap:)` presses the whole surface with no ripple, `PortalButton` keeps its ripple and adds the scale (a loading button now goes through the same path and announces as disabled), `PortalFilterChip` and `PortalStatCard` press too.

- Spring motion tokens. `PortalMotion` carries the M3 Expressive springs (`spatialFast/Default/Slow`, `effectsFast/Default/Slow`) as `SpringDescription`s plus `PortalSpringCurve`, a `Curve` that plays a spring to its settle time and exposes that as `duration` for implicit animations. `standard` is now the effects spring (never overshoots, so nothing that lerps opacity, shadows or padding breaks) and `emphasized` the spatial one; page transitions, the stagger reveal, bottom nav, segmented tabs and filter chip run on them.

- Plus Jakarta Sans ships in the package (`assets/fonts/`, OFL). `PortalTypeScale.fontFamily` defaults to it and `buildPortalTheme` sets it as `ThemeData.fontFamily`, so an app gets the fleet typeface with no font of its own; a per-style family in `PortalTextStyles` still wins.
